# Talk track: six-month results for the donor

About 375 spoken words: **3 to 4 minutes** at a calm pace with pauses for each chart, inside the five-minute limit. One chart on screen per finding. The numbers are in the charts; the talk says what they mean.

Timings are a guide: practise with a stopwatch and cut, never rush.

---

## Opening (30 seconds)

> Thank you for your time. In the last six months, 370 children in Kitui, Marsabit and Turkana joined the programme, and together they have gained almost 400 kilos. I want to show you three things we learned, and what we would like to do next because of them.

*(No chart yet. Let them hear the one big number before anything else.)*

## Finding 1: counties (1 minute 15 seconds)

**On screen:** `charts/county_results.png`

> Children in Kitui gained the most weight. Turkana fell behind.
>
> When we first saw this, we worried that something in Turkana wasn't working. So we looked closer. Families in Kitui came to about four sessions in every five. In Turkana it was just over half. We think distance is a big part of it, but our records don't yet show how far each family travels, so that is something we will start measuring.
>
> When we compare only the children who came regularly, Turkana's children did almost as well as Kitui's. So the programme works in Turkana. The families there just can't get to it as often.

*Pause. Let them look at the chart.*

## Finding 2: attendance (1 minute 15 seconds)

**On screen:** `charts/attendance_and_gain.png`

> This leads to the most important thing we learned. The more sessions a child came to, the more weight they gained, at every step.
>
> A child who came to almost every session gained clearly more each month than a child who came to fewer than half.
>
> Every session is a meal we watch the child eat, a weight check, and a conversation with the parent. When a family misses sessions, the child misses all three. So getting families through the door is the single biggest thing we can improve.

## Finding 3: age (1 minute)

**On screen:** `charts/age_groups.png`

> The last finding is about age. The youngest children, under two, gained weight fastest, faster than older children who came just as often.
>
> This is the age when a child's body catches up most quickly. The same session does more for a baby than for a ten-year-old. So in the next phase we want to find and enrol children under two first.

## The ask (45 seconds)

> So we would like your support for three things:
>
> First, transport or outreach sessions closer to home for Turkana families.
> Second, a follow-up call to any family that misses two sessions in a row, in all three counties.
> Third, enrolling children under two first in the next intake.
>
> Thank you. I'm happy to take any questions.

---

## Reminders for myself

- Don't read the chart out loud. The donor can see that Kitui's bar is longest. Say what it **means**.
- One chart at a time. Take it down before moving on.
- If asked for a number, give it, then return to what it means.
- Done means the listener can repeat the main point back in their own words: *"Kids who come more often gain more weight, so help Turkana families get there, and start with babies."*
